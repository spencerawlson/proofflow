How it works

Three people, one smooth flow.

Dispatch plans the day. Drivers work the route. Customers watch it happen. Here’s the whole thing, end to end.

For the business

Dispatch: plan the day in minutes

1 Create the route

  1. Enter the day’s stops — addresses, order numbers, delivery windows, special instructions.
  2. Assign the route to a driver. Stops sync to their phone in seconds.
  3. Adjust on the fly: add a stop, reorder, reassign — the driver’s list updates immediately.

2 Watch it happen

  1. The live map shows every driver, their completed stops, and what’s next.
  2. Exceptions surface instantly — failed delivery, recipient not home — with notes and photos.
  3. At day’s end, every stop has a record. Disputes get answered with evidence, not memory.
For the driver

The driver: stop by stop, proof by proof

No training course required. Open the route, tap the next stop, deliver, capture proof, move on.

1

Open today’s route

The full stop list is on the phone, in order, with addresses and instructions. Tap a stop to navigate.

2

Deliver and capture proof

At the door: snap a photo of the drop, get the recipient’s signature on the phone. Timestamp and GPS are captured automatically.

3

Log what didn’t work

Nobody home? Package refused? Tap the reason, add a photo and a note. The exception is recorded with the same rigor as a delivery.

No connection, no problem. Records are captured on the device and upload automatically when the driver is back in coverage. Proof is never lost to a dead zone.

For the customer

The customer: informed, not anxious

Customers don’t need an app or an account. One link, in a browser, tells them everything.

A link, not a login

Tracking arrives by text or email. It opens in any browser — nothing to download, no account to create.

Live tracking

They see their delivery’s position and progress in real time — the “where’s my order?” call answers itself.

Proof of arrival

The moment the stop is complete, the customer gets a confirmation — with the proof attached.

The result

Every stop ends the same way: on record.

Photo. Signature. Timestamp. GPS. Searchable forever. That’s the whole product — and it changes the economics of every delivery you run.
