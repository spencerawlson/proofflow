The problem

Deliveries go wrong. Proof settles it.

When there’s no evidence of what happened at the door, the business pays — in refunds, in wasted hours, and in trust.

Disputed deliveries

The customer says the package never arrived. Your driver says it was delivered. With nothing on record, you eat the refund — or the argument.

The phone never stops

“Where’s my order?” calls pull dispatch off real work. Without live tracking, every status question becomes a manual investigation.

Paper proof is dead weight

Paper delivery slips get lost, wet, and illegible — then filed in a drawer where nobody can find them when a dispute lands weeks later.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is ProofFlow really free?

Yes — the free plan is the core of ProofFlow, not a trial. It covers proof of delivery: photos, e-signatures, GPS timestamps, and searchable records. ProofFlow is being rebuilt from scratch, and early access is free. Paid tiers for larger fleets will arrive later, but the free plan stays free.

What does a driver need to use it?

A smartphone. The driver opens the route, works stop by stop, and captures a photo and signature at each delivery. Records upload automatically whenever there’s a connection — no paperwork, no end-of-day data entry.

Does the customer have to install anything?

No. Customers receive a tracking link by text or email. It opens in any browser and shows where their delivery is and a confirmation once it arrives. Nothing to download, no account needed.

How is customer data handled?

Delivery records — photos, signatures, GPS data — belong to your business and are used to run and improve the service. We don’t sell personal data, and we don’t share delivery records with advertisers. The full details are in our privacy policy.

When does ProofFlow launch?

ProofFlow is relaunching — the new version is being built from scratch right now. Join the early access list and you’ll be among the first fleets on board when it opens, with your feedback shaping the product.

Can I export my delivery records?

Your records are searchable by address, date, driver, and order — so finding the proof for a disputed delivery takes seconds. Bulk export and reporting options are planned as part of the relaunch; early access members will help define exactly what they need.
