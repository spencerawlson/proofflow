Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. This generator runs entirely in your browser — no account, no app to install, no limits, no catch.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Records are created and stored only on your own device — your photos and signature never leave it. If you clear your browser data, your saved records go with it.

Does it work offline once loaded?

Once the page is loaded in your browser, the tool keeps working without a connection. Your phone’s GPS needs a signal for the location stamp, and nothing is ever sent anywhere either way.

Can I use it for my business every day?

That’s exactly what it’s for — a quick, free way to prove deliveries and completed work. When you’re ready for dispatch, routes, and a whole team, the full ProofFlow app is on its way. Join the early access list and we’ll let you know.

Can I use it for proof of work, not just deliveries?

Yes — switch the record type to Work completed at the top of the tool. You get a step list: describe each step of the job, snap a photo as each one is done, and the record checks the steps off in order. It’s built for field service, contractors, cleaners, and installers — get the client’s signature and stamp the time and location, same as a delivery.
