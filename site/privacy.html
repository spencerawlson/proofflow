Privacy policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This privacy policy explains what information ProofFlow collects when you visit our website or use our early access program, and how that information is used. ProofFlow is an independent software project based in Montréal, Canada.

1. Information you provide to us

  • Contact and early access requests. When you submit our contact or waitlist forms, your email app sends us the details you enter: name, email address, company name, fleet size, and your message. We use this only to respond to you and to manage the early access program.
  • Communications. If you email us directly, we keep your email address and message so we can reply.

2. Delivery data (the ProofFlow app)

ProofFlow is proof-of-delivery software. The app handles operational delivery records on behalf of the delivery business using it:

  • Delivery photos, recipient signatures, timestamps, and GPS coordinates captured at delivery stops
  • Route and stop information (addresses, order references, driver assignments)
  • Exception records (failed delivery, recipient not home, refused, damaged)

These records belong to the delivery business. They are used to provide the service — proof, tracking, history, and reporting — and are not sold to third parties. We do not use delivery photos, signatures, or recipient information for advertising.

3. Information collected automatically

  • Basic analytics. Our hosting provider may log standard, anonymized technical data such as pages visited, browser type, and approximate region, used to keep the site running and understand which content is useful.
  • Cookies. We use a minimal set of cookies for site functionality. Advertising partners described below may set their own cookies; see the advertising section.

4. Advertising — Google AdSense

ProofFlow is free for the businesses that use it, and this website is supported in part by advertising. We use Google AdSense to serve ads on this site.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor’s use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

Our ads.txt file at proofflow.me/ads.txt declares our authorized advertising relationship.

5. How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share information only in these cases:

  • With service providers that help operate the site (e.g., hosting), bound by confidentiality and data-protection obligations
  • With advertising partners as described above, via their own cookies and policies
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of ProofFlow, our users, or the public

6. Data retention

Contact and waitlist emails are kept for as long as needed to respond and manage early access, then deleted on request. Delivery records are retained for the delivery business according to their needs and applicable law.

7. Your rights

You may ask us at any time to access, correct, or delete the personal information you provided to us (for example, your waitlist entry), by emailing contact@proofflow.me. We will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Children

ProofFlow is a business product and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product and site evolve. The “last updated” date at the top will change, and material changes will be noted here. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@proofflow.me.
