Features

Everything a delivery stop should produce.

ProofFlow captures the evidence, tracks the fleet, and notifies the customer — automatically, on every stop.

Proof

Photo proof of delivery

A timestamped photo of every drop-off, attached to the stop’s record permanently. When a customer says “it never arrived,” you answer with a photo — not an argument.

  • Timestamp and GPS coordinates captured automatically with each photo
  • Multiple photos per stop for damaged or partial deliveries
  • Photos stored with the delivery record — retrievable months later
Proof

E-signature capture

The recipient signs right on the driver’s phone. A signature on record is the fastest way to end a “I never received it” claim — and it looks professional at the door.

  • Sign with a finger or stylus — no paper, no scanning
  • Signature saved alongside the photo, timestamp, and GPS location
  • Optional name capture for a complete handover record
Proof

GPS timestamps & geolocation

Every record is stamped with where and when it happened — automatically, with nothing for the driver to type. The proof isn’t just “delivered”; it’s delivered here, at this time.

  • Coordinates captured at the moment of delivery
  • Flags stops completed far from the delivery address
  • An audit trail you can actually defend in a dispute
Tracking

Live fleet tracking map

Watch every driver move stop to stop on a live map. Dispatch sees the whole operation at a glance; “where’s my driver?” stops being a question you have to answer from memory.

  • Real-time driver positions and completed stops
  • Route progress: how far along each driver is, and what’s next
  • Share tracking links with customers so they can follow along
Records

Delivery history & searchable records

Every stop becomes a permanent record: photos, signature, GPS, timestamps, driver, order. Search by address, date, driver, or order number and pull up the proof in seconds — even months later.

  • Full record per stop, organized by route and date
  • Search across everything — no digging through paper or spreadsheets
  • A dispute folder that builds itself, every single day
Customers

Instant customer notifications

The moment a delivery is complete, the customer knows. A tracking link by text or email, a live map while the driver is en route, and a confirmation with proof when it arrives.

  • Live tracking link — opens in a browser, no app install
  • Delivery confirmation the moment the stop is complete
  • Fewer “where’s my order?” calls to your dispatch
Exceptions

Exception handling that protects you

Not every stop goes cleanly. ProofFlow records the ones that don’t: failed delivery, recipient not home, refused, wrong address, damaged on arrival. The exception is documented — with the same photos, timestamps, and GPS — so a failed delivery never becomes your word against theirs.

  • Log exceptions in seconds with preset reasons and notes
  • Photo evidence of attempted delivery, door tags, or damage
  • Automatic re-attempt scheduling and customer notification
Insight

Reports & exports

Your delivery data, put to work. Daily completion summaries, exception rates, per-driver and per-route breakdowns — the numbers that tell you where the operation is leaking money.

  • Daily and weekly delivery summaries for dispatch
  • Exception and re-attempt reporting
  • Records exportable for your own systems and accountants
