About

Proof software for the people who move things.

ProofFlow is an independent software project from Montréal, built on a simple belief: the delivery businesses doing the hard work deserve professional tools — free.

What ProofFlow is

ProofFlow is a free proof-of-delivery app and live fleet tracker for delivery businesses. Couriers, local fleets, furniture and appliance deliverers, food and beverage distributors, field service teams — anyone whose work ends at a doorstep or a loading dock.

The product is straightforward: dispatch assigns stops, drivers capture photo proof and signatures stop by stop, every record is stamped with time and GPS, customers follow along live, and the business keeps a complete, searchable history of everything delivered.

Why it exists

Disputed deliveries are a tax on honest businesses. A customer says the package never arrived; the driver says it was delivered; with nothing on record, the business pays. Paper delivery slips — lost, wet, illegible — were the industry’s answer for decades. They weren’t good enough then, and they aren’t good enough now.

ProofFlow exists to give small and mid-sized delivery operations the same quality of proof that the giant carriers have — without the enterprise price tag. The free plan is the core product, not a teaser.

Where it stands

ProofFlow is relaunching. The original version proved the idea; the new version is being rebuilt from scratch, properly — and it’s being built in the open with the fleets who will use it. That’s why there’s an early access list instead of a download button: we’d rather onboard teams in waves and get it right than ship something half-finished.

If you run deliveries, join the waitlist. Your routes will shape what ProofFlow becomes.

How it’s funded

ProofFlow is free for the businesses that use it. The site is supported by advertising — you may see ads on these pages, and paid tiers for larger fleets will arrive later. What will never change: the free plan stays free, and your delivery records are never sold.

We read everything. If you run deliveries and have thoughts on what proof software should do, we want to hear them.
